# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

## International shipping
We ship to the United States, Canada and the European Union only. We do not ship to other countries.

## Delayed orders
An order is delayed when it has not arrived 10 business days after it was placed. Delayed orders are investigated by the carrier team; the agent shares the latest status from the order record.
