# Refunds and returns

## Return window
Physical items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Returns after 30 days are not accepted.

## Digital goods
Digital goods such as e-books are non-refundable once downloaded. If a digital file is corrupt, we replace it instead of refunding it.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, the customer gets a full refund or a free replacement within 90 days of delivery. Ask the customer for a photo of the damage. The customer does not need to return a damaged item.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the return. We never refund in cash or gift cards, and agents may not offer credits beyond the order total.
